Small talk

The first two minutes with someone new, and how to keep it going.

6 phrases

Indonesian + 10 local languages

About 4 minutes

Phrases for small talk

Local Language

  1. 01

    Kenalin, aku Sam.

    “Nice to meet you, I am Sam.”

    keh-NAH-lin AH-koo Sam

    Opening

  2. 02

    Asalnya dari mana?

    “Where are you from?”

    ah-SAL-nyah dah-ree MAH-nah

    Asking

  3. 03

    Sudah lama di sini?

    “Have you been here long?”

    SOO-dah LAH-mah dee SEE-nee

    Asking

  4. 04

    Seru, ya, di sini.

    “It is fun here, is it not.”

    seh-ROO yah dee SEE-nee

    Small talk

  5. 05

    Boleh minta kontak?

    “Can I get your contact?”

    BO-leh MIN-tah KON-tak

    Closing

  6. 06

    Sampai ketemu lagi!

    “See you again!”

    SAM-pai keh-TEH-moo LAH-gee

    Closing

CULTURE TIPS

Dari mana is friendly

Being asked where you are from is warmth, not an interrogation. Answer short and ask it back.

Kakak and adik

Call someone slightly older kakak and someone younger adik. It softens a first meeting.

Stuck on one of these?

Ask Gali how to say it your way, or whether it sounds polite.
