The Story

Phrasebooks teach the wrong sentences.

Phrasebooks teach “Where is the train station?”. What you need every day is asking a price, saying “not spicy”, and saying thanks like a person. We built Gaulingo after watching exchange students go quiet at the stall next door.

“The grammar was never the hard part. Knowing which hand to use was.”
